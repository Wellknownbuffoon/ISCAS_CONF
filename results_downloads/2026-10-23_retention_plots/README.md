Figure 2: test error versus retention time. Panel (a) compares NSR-HWA and HWA under the same DriftSlice B=1 correction. Panel (b) compares correction methods with HWA training held fixed. Bands are mean ±2 sample SD across three training-repetition means, each averaged over 30 device realizations. Bands are not 95% confidence intervals. Timestamps are equally spaced on the displayed axis.

Figure 3: measured accuracy differences, in percentage points. The upper-right triangle shows 20 seconds; the lower-left shows five years. Comparisons are NSR versus HWA with compensation held fixed, and DriftSlice versus GDC-safe with training held fixed. This is a method comparison, not a full hyperparameter sweep. Colors do not denote statistical significance.
